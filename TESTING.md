# Accessibility Test Protocol

This 15-minute pass is for low-vision and screen-reader-assisted play.

1. Open the game at 100% zoom and confirm the main menu is readable.
2. Increase browser zoom to 200% and confirm no horizontal scrolling is required.
3. Start a run using only the keyboard.
4. Pause with Escape and toggle high contrast, reduced motion, and large HUD.
5. Resume and confirm the HUD is easier to read with large HUD enabled.
6. Reach the merchant and open the shop with E.
7. Navigate shop items with arrow keys.
8. Buy an item with Enter or Space.
9. Leave the shop with E or Escape and confirm focus returns to the game.
10. Take damage and confirm the event is announced by your screen reader.
11. Reach a checkpoint and confirm the checkpoint announcement.
12. Die and confirm the game-over dialog receives focus.

Please report failures with browser, operating system, assistive technology, zoom level, and the exact step that failed.
